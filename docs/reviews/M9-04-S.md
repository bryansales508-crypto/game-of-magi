## What checks out
- **Start refusals** (`GripService.luau:64-65, 276-302`): a victim who is not Knocked, or is Carried, Jailed or already Gripped; someone gripping themselves; a victim out of reach (server positions); a gripper who is Knocked, Stun, TrueStun, Jailed, Carrying, Carried, Gripping or Gripped; a dead character or a missing root part. A second grip on the same victim is refused by the `Gripped` status, so two grips can't race to the same kill.
- **Gating:** `Gripping`/`Gripped` are added to BLOCKS_RUN/DASH (MovementService), BLOCKS_ATTACK/BLOCK_START, and ENDS_BLOCK/INTERRUPTS_ATTACK (CombatService). The gripper is pinned by the `Gripping` modifier, which is cleared in `endGrip` (the single teardown path every stop and completion goes through). That path also clears statuses, attributes and connections.
- **Interrupts:** a landed hit (`HitLanded` fires next to `interruptAttack` in `ApplyHit`, CombatService.luau:505-511); gripper Knocked/Stun/TrueStun/Jailed (StatusService.Changed); death, character removal or respawn (Died/AncestryChanged, plus a per-tick identity check); leaving (PlayerRemoving); BreakDistance; `GripStop`; the victim no longer Knocked; the victim Carried or Jailed.
- **Kill:** completes only from `Tick` behind the `byGripper[grip.gripper] == grip` identity guard, so nothing can kill after a stop. Setting `Health = 0` goes through LifeService's ordinary `Died:Once` path (costs a life; the last life runs the scene). The victim's Knocked status stays on until death. Hunts are dropped on death by the existing `WatchDeath`.
- **Rewards:** `SettleByGrip` → `GripPayout` pays only the hunter whose hunt targets this victim, and only while the victim is wanted (`Settle` checks `isWanted` and zeroes the bounty, so it pays once). It shares `collect` with the jailing, giving a **Black** deed, no jail, and auto-dropping other hunters. `Grip.BlackRukh` is added on every player grip. An NPC gripper has `gripperPlayer = nil`, so no reward or deed calls happen. No bounty is ever added.
- **Remotes:** `GripStart` takes an integer userId ≥ 1 at 2/s; `GripStop` 3/s. `GripState` carries only names, userIds and progress for the two parties.
- **Dummies:** the grip branch sits after `Sequence(IsKnocked, Wait)`, so a knocked dummy never grips. It honours `gripsOnKnockout` and skips Carried, Jailed, or Gripped-by-another targets. AttackRange (4) is within ReachStuds (5). Trainers (`BuildDummy`) are unchanged.
- **Portraits:** the clone is made with Archivable flipped only around `:Clone()`. Scripts, Sounds, Animator, Tools, prompts and particles are stripped, all parts anchored and non-colliding, and it sits in a `WorldModel` with its own Scriptable Camera. Rebuilds are token-debounced (`PortraitSettleSeconds`), and an ordinary sync rebuilds nothing (self-tested). `Destroying` clears the map entry and all connections, and the clone goes down with the poster.
- `--!strict`, `Log` only, short comments. Config edits are limited to `Config.Bounty` (Poster portrait knobs + `Grip`) and `Config.Npc.Target`, all frozen. SYSTEMS.md §19 and TINKER.md match the code.
