## Checklist

1. **Carry authority: changes needed (R1, R2).** `Decide` is pure and refuses self, not-Knocked, out of reach (6 studs, nil root = refused), a target that is Jailed/Carrying/Carried, and a carrier that is Knocked/Stun/TrueStun/Jailed/Carrying/Carried. Both characters must be alive. The `WeldConstraint` goes from the carrier's torso to the target's root, with collisions off and restored per part. Network ownership goes to the carrier and back to the target on drop; both calls are pcall'd and skipped when anchored. Drop paths: the manual remote, `.carry drop`, the carrier knocked (`StatusService.Changed`), either side dying or being removed (Died/AncestryChanged, deferred), either side leaving (synchronous on `PlayerRemoving`), the knockout ending (refresh), and jail delivery (placed at the cell). Each one disconnects every connection and destroys the weld. The Carried player stays Knocked through the 0.5 s refresh (re-apply doesn't re-fire `Changed`, so there's no re-ragdoll), apart from R1, and gets 4 s of grace after a drop. `BLOCKS_RUN`/`BLOCKS_DASH`/`BLOCKS_ATTACK`/`BLOCKS_BLOCK_START` include all three statuses. `ENDS_BLOCK`/`INTERRUPTS_ATTACK` include Carrying and Jailed. The speed modifier is set and cleared, and run is turned off on pick-up. The statuses are attribute-only with no duration. Remotes: `CarryPickUp` has an integer UserId ≥ 1 and a rate of 2/s; `CarryDrop`/`JailDeliver` take no args at 2/s. The `byCarrier`/`byTarget` tables are cleared on every drop.
2. **Jail resolution and containment: pass (R3, R5, R6).** The structure is found by folder, dropoff and spawn names only. Bounds are the rotated-box AABB of every BasePart under `LargeCell`, plus a 2-stud margin. There's no `Jail1` lookup, and the self-test uses 8 same-named, rotated walls and a decoy folder. The placeholder warns once when it's made. `canDeliver` and `Deliver` both require `Progress.Wanted == prisoner.UserId`. `Deliver` also requires carrying, the dropoff box within 8 studs, and `IsWanted`, and it reads the bounty before `ClearForImprisonment` (which zeroes it before paying, so it pays once). The order is: pay, drop at `JailSpawn`, `Imprison` (drops the prisoner's own hunt, then `JailUntil = now + SentenceSeconds(bounty)` clamped 60..600, then `Jailed`, the jump lock and the teleport). The jump lock saves the humanoid's own values once per humanoid (weak keys) and restores them on release or `Replaced`. A new character after respawn records its own values. Nothing else on the server writes jump values except AgeService's death freeze, which doesn't conflict. The 1 s tick only does work for players with `JailUntil ≠ 0`: an AABB test and a pivot when outside. Death while jailed: StatusService clears statuses on `Died`, the spawn override pivots the new character to the cell before `CharacterPlaced`, and `Ready` and the tick re-apply `Jailed` and the lock. Players who aren't jailed get `nil` from the override, so their spawn is unchanged. A rejoin with a future `JailUntil` goes to the cell and republishes the `JailUntil` attribute. Release: the save goes to 0, the attribute to 0, `Jailed` is removed, jump is restored, the player is pivoted to `QarzinSpawn`, and one `BountyNotice` is sent.
3. **Hunter death: pass (R7 minor).** `Humanoid.Died:Once` is on the hunter's humanoid at Take and stored in `hunt.connections`. `Drop` disconnects every watcher before destroying the Tool, so the later Backpack clear can't drop it a second time. Knockouts set Health to 1 and never reach `Died`.
4. **Saves: pass (R4 dev-only).** `Progress.JailUntil` is in the template and in migration v5. It's written only by `ApplyImprison` (floored) and `ApplyRelease`. `Replaced` (Wipe/NewLife always reset to the template) clears the `JailUntil` attribute, the `Jailed` status and the lock. CarryService writes nothing to the save.
5. **Tests and style: mostly pass (R8, R9, R10).** Covered: every pick-up refusal; carried stays Knocked past the original timer; run, dash and attack gated on, then off again after the drop; grace after a drop; a knocked carrier drops the target; each delivery refusal (pure); the sentence clamp at both ends; the save timestamp and rejoin maths; release once; the real jail structure; bounds with the margin; dropoff reach; Imprisonment pays once (M9-01 test). Every file is `--!strict` and uses `Log` only, with no deprecated APIs and short present-tense comments. SYSTEMS 18 and TINKER match the code. check.sh has 0 errors.
