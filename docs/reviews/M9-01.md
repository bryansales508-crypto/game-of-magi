## Checklist

1. **Save v5: pass.** Migration 5 sets `Wanted = nil` and `JailUntil = 0`. It's idempotent, guards a partial save, and the template matches (`JailUntil = 0`, `Wanted` absent). `Wanted` is written only by Take/Drop and is cleared on load; `JailUntil` isn't read yet. `ApplyAdd` floors and clamps at 0. Interception payouts are already whole and positive, so the amounts are unchanged. `Replaced` drops the stale hunt, clears `Wanted`, republishes `Bounty`/`WantedTarget` and drops hunters of a now-unwanted player. The old migration tests now expect the current version.
2. **Server authority: pass, apart from R2.** `BountyToolEquipped` is validated (boolean) and rate-limited (4/1 s), and it's ignored unless it matches the server-created Tool's real parent. Poster prompts are wired on the server. Take refuses: self, below Adventurer (index 3 = Adventurer, checked by a self-test), already holding one, offline/unloaded/leaving, not wanted, no Backpack, no character. One hunt per hunter. The Tool can't be dropped, and any move outside the hunter's Backpack/Character, or destroying it, drops the bounty once. Clients can't duplicate server tools. Drop disconnects its watchers before destroying the Tool, so the drop doesn't run twice. `BountyState` only carries `lastKnown` (the take snapshot, reach refreshes, and R1). Note that Roblox replicates every character's position to every client anyway, so these rules are game rules, not secrecy.
3. **Tracking: changes needed (R1, R2).** The snapshot on take, reach refresh, reveal at 80, lose after 6 s, unequipped silence apart from one clearing update, and the single 1 s loop that returns early for unequipped hunters all match the plan.
4. **Posters: pass (R3, R8 minor).** One per wanted online player, sorted (bounty, then name) and capped at 8. Posters are reused and repositioned, and anyone no longer wanted or leaving is destroyed. Prompt connections die with their poster. Syncs are deferred and coalesced, and run on bounty changes, leaves, and the 20 s city refresh, never every tick. The placeholder and poster folders are swept on Start.
5. **Rewards: pass.** `Settle` zeroes the bounty before paying, so it pays at most once. Copper goes through `GiveCopperValue`, Magoi is `min(50, 25 + floor(b/4))` through `AddMagoi`, and 1 Gold through `AddDeed`. It refuses (returns nil) for no bounty, prisoner == hunter, or a missing save, and drops every hunter (the collector gets "Bounty collected."). Interception now routes through `BountyService.Add(attacker, payout, ...)` in the same place with the same amount. There's no require cycle: MissionService requires BountyService, not the other way round.
6. **Dev/tests/docs: pass.** `.bounty <n> [player] | list | take | drop | clear` is registered through DevService, so the central `IsDev` check applies. Self-tests cover the threshold, every Take refusal, HuntersOf, Settle once plus the cap, the v4 to v5 migration, CityAt inside and outside a region, poster create/update/remove/cap, Tool loss/destroy once, and the tracking transitions. Every new file is `--!strict`, logging goes through `Log` only, and there are no deprecated APIs. SYSTEMS section 17 documents the payloads, attributes and save fields for M9-03, and TINKER has the knobs and commands.

## For Bryan
- The bounty system is solid: posters on a real board, a "Bounty: name" tool, safe saves, and a payout that can only happen once. The server checks everything.
- Two small fixes are needed in tracking. Losing a target should send you back to where you last saw them, not show you where they are now. Toggling the tool shouldn't speed up the "lose them" timer.
- Two quick extras: the stand-in board might float in the air until you place the real `BountyBoard`, and refusal messages currently go through the dev console channel.
- One question for you: should a hunter who dies lose the bounty? Right now they do. Being knocked out doesn't lose it.
