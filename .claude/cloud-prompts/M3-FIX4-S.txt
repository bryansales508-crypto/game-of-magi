BRYAN'S DESIGN (approved 2026-09-28): rock-paper-scissors combat, the basis for every weapon. Every weapon is a fighting style: a chain of hits, each Light or Heavy, plus traits. Shared rules:
1. Light: fast, cannot be interrupted, small damage, NO knockback. A landed light puts the target in LightStun for the hit's lightStun seconds: they cannot block or parry, but they CAN attack and dash.
2. Heavy: slow, readable wind-up (the client slows it), big damage, big block drain. Interruptible: any hit landing on a combatant during a Heavy's wind-up cancels their attack (AttackCancelled reason=interrupted). Hits during a Light's wind-up do NOT interrupt.
3. Cancel: the next unlanded hit can be cancelled until its cancelUntil time (seconds since the swing started; for a Light equal to its windupAt, for a Heavy partway into the wind-up). What it cancels INTO is the style's cancelInto trait: "Any" (the player may block, jab or dash next, no recovery) or "Jab" (the server immediately starts the style's first light as a new attack flagged feint=true). The cancel highlight event stays.
4. Feint jab on block: if a feint hit is BLOCKED (not parried), the attacker gets feintRecoverySeconds of a Recovery status (cannot attack, block or dash) so the blocker can punish. If it lands, normal.
5. Parry: unchanged (window, cooldown, full Stun on the parried attacker); beats lights and heavies alike.
6. Knockback by count: per combatant HitCount attribute, +1 per landed hit from any source; on the Combat.Knockback.everyHits (6) hit apply Combat.Knockback.impulse away from the attacker, emit KnockedBack {target}, reset the count; the count also resets after resetAfterSeconds (3) without being hit and on knockout. No other knockback: remove the per-hit knockback.
7. Bleed: a Bleed status with stacks (data.stacks, max bleed.maxStacks), applied by hits with bleedStacks > 0; HealthService ticks bleed.damagePerStackPerSecond per stack each second for bleed.seconds (refreshed on a new stack); never knocks out by itself (floors at 1 HP). Attribute Bleed = stack count.
8. Style traits also include speedMult (walk and run) and dashCooldownMult; MovementService applies them through a Style modifier and a scaled dash cooldown (SetStyle(character, styleName)).
