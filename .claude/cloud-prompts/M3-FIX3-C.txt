## Bryan's design (2026-09-28)
One click = one fist animation (`rbxassetid://125726557974534`, already `Fist` in CombatController) containing TWO punches, right then left. The animation carries events (markers): a wind-up and a hit for each punch. The client sends one hit report per hit marker. During a wind-up, if the attacker has been hit, the attack stops. Right-click cancels ONLY before the first wind-up marker; after that the swing carries through. A cancel briefly highlights the player (the old "Cancel" highlight, `character.Cancel`, a Highlight cloned by CharacterService from `ReplicatedFirst.VFX.MiscEffects.Cancel`) so the enemy sees it. The server (M3-FIX3-S, built in parallel) validates everything.
