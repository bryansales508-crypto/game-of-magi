## Bryan's rules (2026-09-29)
- 1 Gold = 100 Silver = 10,000 Copper. Whole numbers only, never a decimal coin.
- A price is expressed in ONE coin: the largest coin where the rounded amount is at least 1, rounding half up: 40 Copper -> "40 Copper"; 60 Copper -> "1 Silver"; 103 -> "1 Silver"; 149 -> "1 Silver"; 150 -> "2 Silver"; 5,000 Copper -> "50 Silver"; 6,000 -> "1 Gold"; 15,000 -> "2 Gold". (A "1.2 Silver" price can never appear.)
- NO automatic change-making. You pay a Silver price with Silver coins only; if you don't hold enough of that coin the purchase fails with a message (the old flavour lines: the character "says" it through the chat line, as MarketHandler did with the ForcedChat remote; keep that feel but through a new `Net` remote `CoinMessage` (server -> client, one string) and let the client post it). Exchanging coins is the Bank's job (a later milestone); nothing here converts.
- Rewards round the same way as prices (a 130-Copper reward pays 1 Silver, a 40-Copper reward pays 40 Copper), so Silver and Gold prices are reachable before the Bank exists. (Bryan may switch this to Copper-only rewards; keep it one function.)
