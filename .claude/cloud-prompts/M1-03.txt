     `DataService.Get(player): Data?` (the live table or nil), `DataService.WaitFor(player, timeout: number?): Data?`, `DataService.IsLoaded(player): boolean`, `DataService.Loaded: RBXScriptSignal` (fires with the player once the bridge is up), `DataService.Wipe(player)` (resets Data to a fresh template in place, re-runs the first-load setup, refreshes the bridge; used by M1-05's dev command), `DataService.Save(player)` (ProfileStore Save, for dev use).
